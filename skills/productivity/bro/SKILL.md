Restate the message. Same content, plainer words.

- Drop the jargon and the internal names. Say what they mean instead.
- Shorter than the original. Cut what the reader does not need.
- One person, one voice: the way you would explain it at the next desk.

The facts do not change. Same numbers, same caveats, same open questions. When the earlier message was wrong, say so here instead of smoothing it over.

This is not `ketchup`, which reports what changed since the user's last message, and not `in-your-own-words`, which plays the task back before work starts. This one only re-says something already sent.
